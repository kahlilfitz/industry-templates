Skills: research-retrieve -> trend-compare -> contradiction-flag -> provenance-check (Govern) -> insight-brief.
Contract: rtl.consumer-insight-synthesis.v1. Engines: trend_compare (#1 ranking, #2 like-for-like deltas, #3 contradiction flags), provenance_check (#4 rights x geography x expiry vs declared use). Constants mirror references/insight-rules.md; rights register and research index live in config/.

Scope boundary (#5.1): retrieval, comparison, citation - no forecasting, claim approval, causal attribution, or large-tabular-file analysis.
