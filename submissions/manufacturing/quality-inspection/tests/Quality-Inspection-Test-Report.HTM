How these tests were run Method & transparency

The shipped engine code was executed directly on the shipped demo inputs — the same files Cowork loads from the plugin zip. Nothing was mocked.

Objective vs. evidenced. Engine-layer results (statuses, statistics, incidence, severity, disposition, certificate conflicts, watch list, escalations) are objective — the engines were re-run on every scenario and the outputs diffed byte-for-byte against the committed golden files (timestamps stripped), plus two synthetic probes constructed to fire specific rule sections. Behavioral results (intake boundary, refusal under pressure, draft-first, scope decline) are evidenced against the exact Constraints and Escalation text shipped in the plugin's SKILL.md files, quoted in the table below. Each verdict is tagged with its basis.

Rules-to-code cross-mapping. Every engine constant mirrors references/disposition-rules.md by section number (MARGINAL_BAND #1.4, MRB_INCIDENCE_PCT #2.1, BORDERLINE_BAND #4.2, CONFIDENCE_FLOOR #4.1), so every grade in the outputs cites the rule that fired.
