What is objective vs. evidenced. Engine-layer results (failure mode, priority, downtime cost, escalations) are objective: my engine output is diffed byte-for-byte against the committed golden outputs (provenance stripped). Behavioral results (intake boundary, draft-first refusal, override pressure, scope decline) are evidenced against the exact governance text shipped in the plugin's skills and reference files, quoted below. Results are reported honestly — a genuine mismatch would be recorded as a FAIL with the diff.
