## Clause 18.2 Reference restriction
The firm may not identify the client by name in marketing material, credential libraries, case studies or press without a later written amendment.

## Clause 18.5 Outcome figure evidence
Only figures verified in final deliverable evidence may be considered for external reference use.

## Clause 18.8 Internal estimates
Client-supplied internal estimates are not approved for external use unless separately verified and approved.
