## 1. Executive summary
The invoice-cycle redesign pilot reduced average approval cycle time by 8.1% across all 942 invoices processed in the pilot region from March to May.

The approved benefits model estimates GBP 1.1m annual run-rate opportunity. The figure is an estimate built on the same 942-invoice extract and assumes the operating controls are adopted consistently.

## 2. Recommendation
Scale the redesigned approval workflow to the two remaining regions. The recommendation is based on the signed 942-invoice pilot dataset and the sponsor-approved benefits model; the two remaining regions processed 1,100 invoices over the same period, and results there may differ from the pilot.

Benefits depend on adoption, control ownership and exception volumes. They are estimates, not assured outcomes.
