## 1. Executive summary
The proposed field-service scheduling redesign will deliver £4.2m annual savings from year one while improving engineer utilization and customer response times.

## 2. Recipient and purpose
This board draft is prepared for Verdant Arc Services and Cobalt Lenders LLP as named recipients for financing diligence.

## 3. Recommendation
We conclude that Verdant Arc should centralize all regional scheduling roles into one national scheduling hub. Interviews with eight dispatch supervisors showed consistent support for the change, so the conclusion applies across the full 120-person dispatch estate.
