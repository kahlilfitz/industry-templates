# SOW MHG-INV-PILOT-2026 rev 4 - excerpt

## 2.1 Closed deliverable list
Supplier deliverables are limited to current-state assessment, future-state process design, pilot workflow prototype, training kit and go-live readiness report.

## 2.2 Included workshops and interviews
The plan includes two design workshops with Finance Operations leads and AP supervisors and up to eight stakeholder interviews named in Appendix B.

## 2.3 Readiness report executive dashboard
The go-live readiness report includes an executive summary dashboard showing adoption risks, open decisions and launch readiness.

## 2.4 Prototype approver roles
The pilot workflow prototype will represent approval roles for AP, Procurement and Legal for invoice exception routing.

## 3.1 Client data assumption
Client will provide one clean export from ERP-X and TicketFlow by 2026-08-14; re-extracts, reconciliation and schema changes remain Client responsibility.

## 4.1 Data and integration exclusions
Data cleansing, duplicate-vendor reconciliation, additional refreshes, schema remediation and new integrations are excluded.

## 4.2 Workshop, interview and training exclusions
Workshops beyond the two included sessions, stakeholder groups not listed in Appendix B and training sessions beyond the training kit are excluded.

## 4.3 Legal workflow redesign exclusion
Legal or compliance workflow redesign, policy interpretation and approval-authority redesign are excluded from this SOW.

## 6.1 Change-control route
Additional work outside the deliverable list or caused by failed assumptions requires a written change order signed by both parties before performance.
