# Discovery call notes - Sable Ridge Services / Finance Workflow Pilot
Date: 2026-09-14
Attendees: Dana Price (pursuit lead), Lee Morin (engagement manager), Priya Noor (client sponsor), Mateo Chen (client data owner)

The client wants a six-week pilot to document its invoice intake workflow, map current pain
points, design a target workflow and produce an implementation roadmap for a later phase.

Confirmed deliverables:
- Current-state assessment of invoice intake, matching and exception handling.
- Target operating model for roles, controls and workflow states.
- Implementation roadmap with sequenced backlog, dependencies and risk register.

Acceptance:
- Each deliverable will be accepted against the outline agreed in the 2026-09-14 scoping notes.
- Client review period is five business days.
- Rejections must list specific defects against the agreed outline.

Client obligations:
- Mateo Chen owns the test-data extract.
- Test data will be synthetic or masked and will include 120 representative invoice cases.
- Data quality standard: at least 95% of rows populated for vendor, invoice date, amount and exception code.

Milestones:
- Week 1-2: discovery and current-state assessment.
- Week 3-4: target operating model workshops.
- Week 5-6: roadmap and executive readout.
