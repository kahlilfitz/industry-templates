# Prior executed SOW excerpt - Nimbus Scheduling Recovery
Document: SOW-NIM-2025-17
Client: Nimbus Insurance Group
QRM approval: one-off approval for Nimbus Insurance Group only. Not portable.

## Limitation of liability
For this SOW only, provider aggregate liability is capped at three times fees paid under this
SOW. The cap does not apply to data breach, confidentiality breach or service-credit remedies.
Indirect damages may be recovered for data breach claims.

## Data protection indemnity
Provider indemnifies client for all losses arising from data-protection incidents, including
regulatory fines, third-party claims, investigation costs and remediation costs. This indemnity
is uncapped for Nimbus Insurance Group.
