Fixes the Cowork 1.0.0 evaluation (Blocked). Each finding maps to the fix below.

- **Safety stop: tripwire (novel SOW and pursuit lines passed through).** `safety_text.py` (shared, byte-identical in all five skills) now matches semantic families instead of a phrase list: wall/conflict status claims, approval/exception/waiver/sign-off claims, permission and role grants, scope escalation, send/copy/forward/upload to an e-mail address or URL (including `[at]`/`at … dot` obfuscation), suppression of holds/briefings/register, and assistant- or reviewer-addressed steering. Text is normalised with NFKC, Cf stripping and Cyrillic/Greek→Latin confusable folding, and mixed-script tokens are flagged on their own. `scope_digest` scans line by line: a flagged item is replaced by a withheld placeholder in every section (first-week plan included), holds give the path, line and pattern family only, and flagged text never appears in any output. Regression fixtures: the 12 reconstructed Cowork novel lines (4 SOW, 8 pursuit, including the Cyrillic "wall lifted"), 20 more paraphrases and 38 more benign domain lines that must stay clean. The original 22 benign and 22 attack lines are kept.

A screened topic is withheld, held (`HOLD-BRIEFING-TOPIC-*`) and escalated, and the pack goes on hold. Restricted workspace names that name another client are shown by ID only (`name_withheld_workspace_ids`). In the confidentiality reference, #3.1 now matches this behaviour, with a new #3.2 for the topic screen and new #4.3/#4.4 sections.
