  - A cleared asset with no description now gets a finished statement that none is supplied, in place of the pending-review statement it used to inherit.
  - Descriptions that are never rendered (a pending item's, or cleared guidance) are still Closeout text. They are screened beside the items, with their item's title, ID and citations, as an unwritten lesson's record is. A name in them still holds (QA-1 "Closeout engagement for another client"). ### 1.0.2 QA round 2 (on f4a56eb)

A second QA round confirmed four more blocking defects (cases guidance-drafter-turn, guidance-whoever-synthesizes, passive-reviewed, noun-review, admin-status-check and mixed-cleared-split-pending). Two were vocabulary gaps, so the design changes rather than the word lists. Still 1.0.2 (not frozen).

- **QA-102b-A: first-week classification required a consumption verb** (`access_checklist.py`). "The knowledge digest should be reviewed before kickoff" (passive-reviewed) and "A review of prior knowledge is required before kickoff" (noun-review) stayed verbatim while the digest held pending AST-A-001. "Check the practice review status of AST-A-001 with the knowledge management lead" (admin-status-check) was wrongly restated.
  - Fix: the default is inverted. Any item (or adjacent run) that refers to knowledge content - a digest item by the existing exact, fuzzy or split resolution, or a generic prior-knowledge, knowledge-digest, lessons-learned or Closeout-asset reference - is treated as consuming it while the digest holds a pending item, whatever its verb, voice or noun form ("check", "go through", "should be reviewed", "a review of ... is required", "do not read the digest yet"). The consumption-verb list is gone.
  - The only exemption is an item that is clearly administrative about the review itself: every clause is an admin action (schedule, book, request, chase, confirm, check, ask, follow up, attend, escalate ...) whose object is the review, its status, sign-off or approval, or the practice or KM lead, with only plain phrases or a further admin action after it ("ask the KM lead to confirm the review status"). "Check AST-A-001 before kickoff", "Check the digest before kickoff" and any admin item that also says to read or use the content are restated. When in doubt, the item is restated.
  - Exact references to cleared items still win (round-1 D4); an item naming only cleared items stays verbatim. Round-1 outputs are unchanged (benign-review and cleared-similar stay verbatim).
- **QA-102b-B: a run dropped the cleared items it named** ("Read AST-A-002 and AST-A-" / "001 before kickoff" became a pending-only note). `split_refs()` now returns the cleared items the joined run names, and `first_week_rows()` merges the cleared items of every entry in the run, so the note keeps "read only the cleared item Patient access onboarding guide (AST-A-002)" beside the pending AST-A-001 note. A run is skipped when its joined text is administrative about the review. The split generic check now looks for a knowledge-content phrase that crosses an entry boundary, with no verb requirement.
- **QA-102b-C: the stage-guidance classifier was a verb list** (`knowledge_match.py`). "For the drafter, turn the title and citations into a concise patient access onboarding summary." and "Whoever writes the next version should synthesize these citations into a patient access onboarding summary." on a cleared asset rendered as its summary.
  - Fix: guidance is recognised by structure (#2.6): (i) an address to a producer (for the drafter/writer/editor/author/reviewer, whoever/anyone who + a production verb, the next stage/step/version/draft, second person "you" + a modal); (ii) a directive to produce text: an imperative or modal construction (should, must, needs to, is to, to be ...) whose verb produces or transforms text. Text-only verbs (write, draft, summarise, synthesise, condense, rephrase, rewrite ...) count with any object; general verbs (create, turn, convert, make, build, prepare, outline, expand ...) count when the object is text or they turn something "into" it; "into a ... summary/lesson/note/overview" counts on its own. Verb forms are generated (base, -s, past, participle, -ing); (iii) placeholders and drafting rules, as before.
  - Factual descriptions that merely mention drafters, writers or summaries still render ("Checklist used by the drafting team during onboarding", "Summary of the governance cadence", "Onboarding guide written by the delivery team"). Ambiguous descriptions fail toward caution.
- **Duplication check.** The first-week logic exists only in `access_checklist.py` (wall-check and pack-draft) and the description logic only in `knowledge_match.py` (knowledge-match, wall-check and pack-draft); all copies are byte-identical and `tests/check_shared_copies.py` passes.
