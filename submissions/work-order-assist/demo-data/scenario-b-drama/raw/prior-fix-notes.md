# Fix notes - C-500 / intake valve

## FN-701  (2024-04-18, T. Bauer, Mechanical)
Second VK-100 swap on C-500 in six weeks. Reset unloader, checked seat. Ran OK on test.
Flagged the short interval to reliability.

## FN-882  (2024-05-31, A. Farouk, Reliability Engineer)  *** RECOMMENDED CHANGE ***
Root-cause review of the recurring low-discharge / unloading fault on C-500. The standard
VK-100 intake valve kit uses reeds that fatigue prematurely under C-500's continuous instrument-
air duty cycle - this is why it keeps returning after every SOP-COMP-03 swap. The revised kit
**VK-200** (upgraded reeds, direct fit) resolved the identical recurring fault on C-501 (WO-4402)
with no recurrence since. Recommendation: fit **VK-200** on C-500 instead of another VK-100, and
update SOP-COMP-03 to reference VK-200. VK-100 is now marked obsolete / superseded by VK-200.
supersedes_part: VK-100  ->  superseding_part: VK-200
