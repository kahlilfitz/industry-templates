Automated verdicts (state exactly one, spelled exactly as written):
- Ready for human review: every measured bar is met — identity/SHA match, package structure, every
  skill at or above its launch floor, behavioral, trust and safety, security, trigger conflicts, and
  the shipped regression. The only open items are evidence that the two-reviewer human review
  supplies: installed-runtime critical-path runs, human grader-calibration labels, and human spot
  checks. List those under "Pending human-review evidence"; they are not blockers.
- Blocked: evidence is missing that human review cannot supply (for example a stage that did not run,
  or an ambiguous artifact identity), or a gate failed closed for a reason that is not an observed
  plugin defect.
- No-ship: an observed defect, a below-floor score, or a safety or security blocker.

Do not use a bare "Ready", and do not report Blocked only because installed-runtime runs, human
grader-calibration labels, or human spot checks are still to come; those belong in the
"Pending human-review evidence" section. Ready for human review is never a publish signal.

Do not call the plugin published. State that publication remains pending until two independent human reviewers complete the critical-path spot checks.
