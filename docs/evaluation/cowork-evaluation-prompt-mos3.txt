recommendations.html

At the top, state:
- plugin slug {{SLUG}}, version {{VERSION}} and run label {{RUN_LABEL}}
- the expected and observed SHA-256, and `SHA-256 check: MATCH` or `SHA-256 check: MISMATCH`
- `Automated verdict:` followed by exactly one of: Ready for human review, Blocked, or No-ship

Then one HTML table of suggested fixes with exactly these columns:
ID | Severity | Skill or file | Evidence | Suggested change
- Severity is one of: blocker, required, advisory.
  - blocker: a safety, security or guardrail failure, or a hard stop.
  - required: anything that keeps the plugin below the strategy's bar.
  - advisory: an improvement that is not needed to meet the bar.
- Evidence names the report and check or test case, and what was observed.
- Order the rows by the strategy's fix order: hard stop, logic defect, description gap, routing
  conflict, weak output.
- If there is nothing to fix, keep the table with a single row that says so.

The strategy's bar: every skill at or above its launch floor; behavioral pass rate of at least
95% with no guardrail failures; output quality of at least 3.5/5; trigger consistency 3/3;
negative cases stay out; the security scan passes with no medium or high findings; and the trust
and safety gate passes. A guardrail or safety failure is always a stop.

Decide the verdict only from plugin-health.html and behavioral-test-report.html:
- Ready for human review: every bar in the strategy is met, and only the two-tester human spot
  check remains. It is not a publish signal.
- Blocked: the evaluation could not complete, or the ZIP identity is unclear.
- No-ship: any blocker or required fix.
